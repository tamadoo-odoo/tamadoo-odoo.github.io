Give each product category its own discount limits: what the salesperson may give alone and how far the team leader, sales manager or director may go. Quotations above the limits, or below a minimum margin, are approved level by level before they can be confirmed or sent.

The right person approves the right discount

1. Limits per category, levels needed
2. Approved level by level, with reasons

What you get

🗂️

Limits per category

Rules per product, product category (incl. sub-categories) or a default: the salesperson's limit and each approval level's limit.

🪜

Approval levels

Team leader of the sales team, a named user, or any member of a group (e.g. Sales Administrators). Asked lowest first, only as high as the discount needs.

🧱

Bottom price

Optional minimum margin over cost per rule: a line sold below it needs the level you choose.

🏷️

Fair measure

Discounts are measured against the pricelist price, so pricelist deals need no approval. Highest line or weighted per rule.

🔀

Parallel groups

Each rule group of a quotation is approved on its own, in parallel; a level held by the same person is passed at once.

🔒

No surprises

Confirming (and, by default, sending) waits for approval; lines are locked while it runs; above the last limit can be refused outright.

↩️

Reject, withdraw, resubmit

Reject with a reason, the salesperson withdraws to edit, and an approval still counts if the discount did not rise.

🔔

Activities and history

To-dos for every approver, a Discounts to Approve menu and the full approval history on the quotation.

Step by step

Discount rules

One rule per product category (or product, or default).

A rule

Salesperson 10%, Team Leader 15%, Sales Manager 25%, Director 35%, nothing above; below a 15% margin the Sales Manager must approve.

Approval levels

Who approves: the sales team leader, any Sales Administrator, a named director.

A quotation above the limits

The discount against the pricelist per line and a clear banner; Request Approval appears.

Confirming waits

Confirming (or sending) before approval is refused with an explanation.

What needs approval

Office Furniture at 18% needs Team Leader then Sales Manager; Services at 8% only the Team Leader.

Requested

The first level of every group gets a to-do; the lines are locked.

Discounts to Approve

Each approver sees what waits for them.

Level by level

The team leader approved; the Sales Manager is next for furniture, services are done.

Reject with a reason

The salesperson gets the reason as a to-do and can edit the quotation.

Resubmitted and approved

Lowered to 15%: only the team leader again; the services approval still counts. Full history with the reasons.

Confirmed

Once approved, the quotation is confirmed as usual.

Settings

Links to rules and levels, and whether unapproved quotations may be sent.

Set up in 5 minutes

  1. Install the module (depends on sale_management).
  2. Sales → Configuration → Discount Approval → Approval Levels: who approves, in order (Team Leader and Sales Manager are created for you).
  3. Rules: per product category, the salesperson limit and each level's limit; optionally a minimum margin.
  4. Set a team leader on your sales teams if you use that level.
  5. Salespeople click Request Approval on quotations above their limit; approvers use Discounts to Approve.
