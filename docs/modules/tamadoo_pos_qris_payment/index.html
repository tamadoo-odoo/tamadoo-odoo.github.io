About This Module

Accept QRIS payments in your Odoo POS with a single-scan static QR code. Upload your bank-issued QRIS QR code once on the payment method, and the POS shows it automatically — with the exact transaction amount — whenever a customer pays with QRIS. No external API, no internet connection, no extra QR provider needed. Works with all QRIS-certified Indonesian banks and e-wallets (GoPay, OVO, DANA, ShopeePay, all bank apps).

  • Shows your static QRIS QR code on the POS payment screen with the amount to pay
  • Compatible with every QRIS-certified bank and e-wallet in Indonesia
  • Self-contained — no Bank-App / QR-provider configuration required
  • No external API or internet connection required

Step by step

1. Create the payment method

Open Point of Sale → Configuration → Payment Methods and create or open a method, for example “QRIS”.

2. Upload your QRIS code

Switch on Use Static QRIS (Indonesia) and upload the QRIS QR Code Image you received from your bank.

3. Add the method to your point of sale

Open Point of Sale → Configuration → Settings, choose the point of sale and add the method to its Payment Methods.

4. Take a payment

On the POS payment screen, select the QRIS method. The QRIS Payment window shows your QR code and the amount to pay.

5. Confirm

The customer scans the code and pays. Tap Payment Received and validate the order.
