What it does

Apply a single discount to the whole sale order instead of editing every line. Choose a percentage or a fixed amount, click Apply, and you're done.

  • Uses Odoo's native per-line discount — so it flows correctly to the invoice and accounting.
  • Percentage or fixed amount (spread proportionally across the lines).
  • One click to clear it again.

How it looks

Step by step

1. Open the quotation

Open a quotation with its order lines. The Global Discount block is below the lines.

2. Choose type and value

Select Percentage (%) or Fixed Amount and enter the value.

3. Apply

Click Apply Discount to All Lines. The discount is written on every line; a fixed amount is spread over the lines in proportion.

4. Remove it

Click Clear to take the discount off all lines again.

Requirements

  • Odoo 16 / 17 / 18 / 19 / 20 — Community or Enterprise.
