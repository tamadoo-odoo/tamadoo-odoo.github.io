Save a POS order as a Sales quotation in one tap.

What it does

A Quotation button turns the current POS cart into a draft Sales order, keeping the customer and the lines — perfect for business customers who want a written quote before they buy.

Key features

  • One-tap quotation from the POS order.
  • Carries the customer and order lines into a draft Sales order.
  • Continue the sale from the Sales app as usual.

How it looks

The Sales quotation generated from a POS order:

Step by step

1. Build the order

In the POS, add the products and set the customer. Without a customer the quotation is made out to your own company.

2. Create the quotation

Then tap the ⋮ button next to the control buttons to open Actions, then tap Quotation. A message shows the number of the new quotation.

3. Continue in Sales

Open Sales → Orders → Quotations: the draft quotation has the customer and the lines of the POS order. Send or confirm it as usual.

Requirements

  • Odoo 20 — Point of Sale + Sales (point_of_sale, sale_management).
