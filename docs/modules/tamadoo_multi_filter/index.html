Let shoppers tick several values at once in your shop sidebar.

What it does

Adds a reusable multi-select checkbox filter to your eCommerce shop sidebar. Ships with a Category filter out of the box — customers tick several categories and see every matching product at once.

  • Multi-select checkboxes — combine values within a filter (OR) and across filters (AND).
  • In-list search box for long option lists.
  • Selected values shown as removable chips.
  • Selection kept across pagination and sorting.
  • Toggle the whole block from the website Customize panel.
  • Extensible: add a new dimension by overriding one controller hook.

How it looks

1. Multi-select filters in the shop sidebar

2. Tick several values — chips show your selection

3. The grid narrows to matching products

Step by step

1. Install the module

The filter block is switched on as soon as the module is installed.

2. Give your products eCommerce categories

The filter lists eCommerce categories, so your published products need at least one.

3. Open the shop

On /shop the left sidebar shows a Categories block with one checkbox per category and a search box for long lists. On a phone, tap the shop's filter button: the same block is in the panel that opens.

4. Tick and show

Tick as many categories as you like; each one appears as a chip. Click Show results and the grid shows the products of any ticked category.

5. Change or clear

Remove a chip or untick a box and click Show results again, or click Reset to clear the filter. The selection stays while you change page or sorting.

Requirements

  • Odoo 20 — eCommerce (website_sale).
