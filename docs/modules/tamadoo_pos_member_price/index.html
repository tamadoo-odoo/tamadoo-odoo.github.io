Automatically reward your registered members at the Point of Sale.

What it does

Flag a customer as a member on their contact and set a member discount percentage per Point of Sale. When a member customer is selected at the POS, the discount is applied to the whole order automatically and the cashier is notified — no coupons, no manual discounting.

Key features

  • Member flag on every contact.
  • Per-POS member discount %.
  • Fully automatic — applies when the member is selected and to any product added afterwards.
  • Cashier notification confirming the member discount.
  • Set 0% to disable for a given Point of Sale.

How to use — step by step

1. Set the member discount on your Point of Sale

Point of Sale → Configuration → Settings → Pricing → Member Discount.

2. Mark the customer as a member

3. Add products at the POS

4. Select the member customer

5. The member discount is applied automatically

Requirements

  • Odoo 20 — Point of Sale (point_of_sale).
