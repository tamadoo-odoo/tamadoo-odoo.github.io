Capture a reason code for every POS refund.

What it does

Configure a list of refund reason codes. A Refund Reason button in the Actions menu lets the cashier pick the reason in one tap, and every refund reason is recorded in a backend log — so you can analyse why refunds happen. When the cashier presses Refund on a paid order, the reason is asked automatically before payment.

Key features

  • Configurable refund reason codes (ships with sensible defaults).
  • One-tap reason selection at the POS.
  • Backend refund log with reason, order, customer and cashier.

Step by step

1. Set up the refund reasons

Open Point of Sale → Reporting → Refund Reasons. Four reasons come with the module; add your own, drag them into order or switch one off with Active.

2. Start the refund at the register

Tap Orders, choose the Paid filter and select the order. Select the product, enter the quantity to refund and press Refund.

3. Pick the reason

The Refund Reason popup opens by itself, before payment. Tap the reason, then pay the refund as usual. The Refund Reason button in the Actions menu records a reason for the current order at any other moment.

4. Read the refund log

Open Point of Sale → Reporting → Refund Log: one line per refund, with the time, the receipt number, the reason, the point of sale, the customer and the cashier.

Requirements

  • Odoo 20 — Point of Sale (point_of_sale).
