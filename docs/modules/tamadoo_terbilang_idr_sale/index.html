Text and screenshots below are from the Odoo 20.0 version of this module.

Add-on for Terbilang IDR: prints "Terbilang: ... rupiah" under the total of quotations and sales orders. Installed automatically with Sales.

What you get

Automatic

Installed by itself when Terbilang IDR and Sales are both installed.

Same settings

Follows the Terbilang settings of the company: on/off, currencies, capitalisation.

Field

amount_text_idr on sales orders for your own layouts.

Step by step

Quotation in IDR

The total in words under the total of the quotation / sales order PDF.

Setup

  1. Install Terbilang IDR with Sales: this add-on is installed automatically.
  2. Print a quotation in IDR (pricelist in IDR).

Requirements

Odoo 20.0 (Community or Enterprise) with Terbilang IDR (tamadoo_terbilang_idr) and Sales (sale).
