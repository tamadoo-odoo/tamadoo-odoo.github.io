A public digital menu page for your POS, reachable via QR code.

What it does

Publish a mobile-friendly menu page that lists the products available at a Point of Sale with their prices. The page shows a QR code customers can scan to open it on their phone — print it for tables or the counter, no app required.

Key features

  • Public page at /pos/menu/<id> per Point of Sale.
  • Lists POS products with prices, mobile-friendly.
  • Built-in QR code to share / print.
  • No login required for customers.

How to use — step by step

For the admin (backend)

Point of Sale → Configuration → Point of Sale → open your POS. The Digital Menu QR section shows the menu URL and an Open Menu Page button to preview and print it.

For the customer (user view)

Customers scan the QR (or open the URL) on their phone and browse the live menu with prices — no app, no login.

Requirements

  • Odoo 20 — Point of Sale (point_of_sale).
  • QR codes are drawn by reportlab and need its PNG backend: Odoo's Debian/Ubuntu packages include it, on a pip-based Linux install run pip install rl-renderPM (Odoo's requirements.txt lists it for Windows only), otherwise the QR code shows as a broken image.
