What it does

Print a clean PDF summary for a POS session: totals (sales, tax, discount, average basket), a payment breakdown and a product breakdown — ideal for daily close.

Key features

  • Totals: gross sales, tax, discount, order count, average basket.
  • Payment breakdown by method.
  • Product breakdown by quantity and amount.
  • Print from the POS session form.

How it looks

Step by step

1. Open the session

Open Point of Sale → Orders → Sessions and open the session.

2. Print

Click Print Daily Report at the top of the form.

3. Read the report

The PDF shows the summary (orders, gross sales, tax, discount, average basket), the Payment Breakdown per method and the Product Sales with quantity and total.

Requirements

  • Odoo 20 — Point of Sale (point_of_sale).
