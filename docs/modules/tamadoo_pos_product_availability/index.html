A real-time colour-coded stock badge on every product card in the POS.

What it does

Every product card in the POS shows a colour-coded on-hand stock badge so cashiers see availability at a glance, without opening product info or leaving the order screen.

  • Green — comfortable stock (N left).
  • Amber — low stock (5 or fewer left).
  • Red — Out of stock.

The on-hand quantity (qty_available) is loaded straight into the POS data, so badges are live with no extra clicks. Storable products only; zero configuration.

How it looks

Step by step

1. Install the module

There is nothing to configure.

2. Track stock on your products

The badge is shown for products with inventory tracking switched on. Products that are not tracked get no badge.

3. Open the POS

Open or reload the POS session. Every tracked product card shows its on-hand quantity: green “N left”, amber at 5 or fewer, red Out of stock at zero.

Requirements

  • Odoo 20 — Point of Sale (point_of_sale).
  • Products must be storable for an on-hand quantity to exist.
