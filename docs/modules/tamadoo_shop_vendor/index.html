What it does

For marketplaces, distributors and multi-supplier catalogs: shoppers see who supplies each product and can narrow the shop down to the vendors they trust.

  • Vendor on product tiles: one pill per vendor of the product, right below the price.
  • Vendor filter: multi-select Vendor section in the shop sidebar and the mobile filter panel.
  • Combines with categories, attributes, price, tags and search; survives pagination and sorting.
  • Only vendors that supply a product listed in the shop of the current website are offered in the filter.
  • Show on eCommerce switch on the vendor contact keeps a supplier private on all of its products.
  • Enabled per website, off after installation: no vendor name is exposed until you decide to.
  • Multi-website and multi-company aware. Zero JS — pure QWeb & Python.

How to use

1. Enable the features

Website > Configuration > Settings > eCommerce > Vendor on product tiles and Vendor filter.

2. Vendors in the shop

Each tile shows its vendors; the sidebar lists the vendors to filter on.

3. Filter by vendor

Click one or more vendors; Clear vendors resets the filter.

4. Keep a vendor private

Untick Show on eCommerce on the vendor contact, Sales & Purchase tab.

Requirements

  • Odoo 20 — eCommerce (website_sale) and Purchase (purchase).
