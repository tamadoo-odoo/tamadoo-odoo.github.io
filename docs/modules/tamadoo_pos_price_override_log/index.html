Keep an audit trail of every discount applied at the Point of Sale.

What it does

Every order line sold with a discount is recorded automatically — product, quantity, unit price, discount, cashier and customer. Managers get a clear audit trail to spot unusual discounting, grouped by cashier out of the box.

Key features

  • Automatic logging of every discounted order line.
  • Captures product, qty, unit price, discount %, cashier and customer.
  • Backend list under Point of Sale > Reporting > Price Override Log.
  • Grouped by cashier; searchable and filterable.

How to use — step by step

1. Sell with a discount at the POS

2. Validate the order

3. Review the audit log in the backend

Requirements

  • Odoo 20 — Point of Sale (point_of_sale).
