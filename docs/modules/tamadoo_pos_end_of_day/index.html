What it does

A simple wizard to wrap up the day: it summarises the current session (orders, total sales, expected cash), lets you enter the counted cash, and shows the difference instantly. The summary is logged on the session for the record.

Key features

  • Session summary: orders, total sales, expected cash.
  • Enter counted cash, see the difference live (over/short).
  • Optional note; the summary is logged on the session.
  • Under Point of Sale > End of Day.

How it looks

Step by step

1. Open the wizard

Open Point of Sale → End of Day. The latest open session is filled in; pick another one in Session if needed.

2. Check the summary

Orders, Total Sales and Expected Cash are computed from the session.

3. Count the cash

Enter the Counted Cash. The Difference shows at once, in red when the drawer is short. Add a Note if you want.

4. Record

Click Record. The summary, the counted cash and the difference are logged in the chatter of the session.

Requirements

  • Odoo 20 — Point of Sale (point_of_sale).
