Share the credit for a sale between salespeople and teams: every confirmed order gets a split, a manager approves shared deals, and the credit lands per salesperson, team, product and category in Sales Credit reports.

Shared deals, fair credit

1. Rina splits a deal 70 / 30 with Tomi

What you get

🤝

Shared deals

Split an order between several salespeople and teams by percentage or amount, for the whole order or for single lines.

⚡

No extra work for normal orders

Confirming an order credits 100% to its salesperson at once; only real splits wait for a manager (or every split, if you prefer).

✅

Manager approval

Sales managers get a to-do; a split is approved only when the shares add up to the untaxed order amount exactly.

🧮

Credit per order line

Each share is spread over the order lines in proportion (rounding on the last line), so credit is reported by product and category.

🔁

Revisions with history

Order amount changed after approval? The split is flagged; Revise keeps the agreed percentages on the new amount and supersedes the old one.

🚫

Follows the order

Cancelling the order cancels its split; a split badge on the order list shows what still needs attention.

🕰️

Your history too

Orders confirmed before installing get their split from the order list in one action, dated on the order date.

🔒

Private by design

Salespeople see the deals they take part in and only their own credit; managers and 'all documents' users see everything.

📊

Sales Credit reports

Pivot, graph and list by salesperson, team, product category and month, in company currency; My Sales Credit for every salesperson.

Setting

Approve every split manually, or only real splits (default).

Orders from before the install

Orders confirmed before installing have No Split: select them in the order list...

Create Revenue Split

...and run Actions → Create Revenue Split: each is credited to its salesperson on its order date.

Set up in 5 minutes

  1. Install the module (depends on sale_management).
  2. Optional: Sales → Configuration → Settings → Revenue Split to approve every split manually.
  3. Existing orders: select them in Sales → Orders and run Actions → Create Revenue Split.
  4. Confirm orders as usual; open the Revenue Split button on an order to share it.
  5. Sales managers approve splits in Sales → Orders → Revenue Splits.
  6. Read the credit in Sales → Reporting → Sales Credit; salespeople use Sales → Orders → My Sales Credit.
