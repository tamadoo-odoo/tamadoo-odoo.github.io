Send quotations & invoices to customers via WhatsApp — from Odoo.

What it does

Adds a WhatsApp button to quotations and invoices. A compose wizard lets you review the number and message, then send — in one of two modes per company.

  • wa.me Link — zero setup; opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message + a link to download the PDF.
  • Business API (Meta Cloud) — sends the PDF as an attachment automatically, with delivery tracking (Sent → Delivered → Read) via webhook.
  • WhatsApp status on the order/invoice; every send logged in the chatter.
  • Per-company credentials & configuration.

Step by step

1. Switch it on

Open Settings → WhatsApp and tick Enable WhatsApp Send. Choose the Mode: wa.me Link or Business API, and enter the Public Base URL of your Odoo.

2. Business API only: enter the credentials

Fill in Phone Number ID, Access Token and Webhook Verify Token, and set [Base URL]/tamadoo/whatsapp/webhook as the webhook in your Meta app.

3. Click WhatsApp on a quotation or invoice

The WhatsApp button is at the top of the form.

4. Review and send

The wizard shows the mode, the customer's number and a ready-made message. Check the number (country code, no +) and click Send via WhatsApp.

5. What happens next

With wa.me Link, WhatsApp opens in a new tab with the message and a link to the PDF; press send there. With Business API the PDF is sent automatically. The document shows the WhatsApp status and the send is logged in the chatter.

Requirements

  • Odoo 20 — sale, account.
  • wa.me mode: a public HTTPS base URL. Business API: Meta WhatsApp Business credentials + HTTPS webhook.
