What it does

Print a clear end-of-session report for any POS session: which products were sold and in what quantity, the payment breakdown by method, and the session total — ideal for daily reconciliation and the cash drawer.

Key features

  • Per-session product breakdown (quantity + amount).
  • Payment breakdown by method.
  • Session totals and order count.
  • Available from the Print menu on any POS session.

How it looks

Step by step

1. Open the session

Open Point of Sale → Orders → Sessions and open the session.

2. Print

On the form, open the gear menu next to the record name and choose Session Report (it sits under Print when there are several reports). In the list you can tick several sessions and use Print.

3. Read the report

The PDF lists the Products Sold with quantity and amount, the Payments per method and the session total.

Requirements

  • Odoo 20 — Point of Sale (point_of_sale).
